This Privacy Policy explains how Islamic Online collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

## Information We Collect

We collect limited information to operate and improve the site:

- **Usage data**: Pages visited, time spent, and general location (country or city level) through analytics tools.
- **Device data**: Browser type, operating system, screen size, and referral source.
- **Cookies**: Small text files stored by your browser to remember preferences and serve relevant content.

We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers unless you choose to contact us directly.

## Cookies and Advertising

Islamic Online uses Google AdSense and other third-party vendors to serve advertisements. These vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

- [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com)
- [www.aboutads.info](http://www.aboutads.info)

You can also disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

## Analytics

We use analytics services to understand how visitors use the site. This helps us improve content and tools. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

## Interactive Tools

Our interactive tools, including the Qibla direction finder, date converter, Zakat calculator, and tasbih counter, run entirely in your browser. Any data you enter, such as location, dates, or financial amounts, is processed locally and is not sent to or stored on our servers.

## Children's Privacy

Islamic Online is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under privacy laws such as GDPR (European Union) or CCPA (California). These may include:

- The right to know what data is collected about you.
- The right to request deletion of your data.
- The right to object to certain types of processing.
- The right to data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through our [Contact page](/contact). We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

## Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the limited data we collect. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

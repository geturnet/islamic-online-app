## All prayer times

Muslims perform five obligatory prayers each day at specific times determined by the position of the sun. Each prayer has a defined window during which it must be performed.

| Prayer | Time window | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fajr** | Dawn until sunrise | The morning prayer, performed before the sun rises. It marks the beginning of the day. |
| **Sunrise** | Moment the sun rises | Not a prayer time, but marks the end of Fajr and the beginning of the prohibited time for prayer. |
| **Dhuhr** | After midday until afternoon | The noon prayer, performed after the sun passes its zenith. |
| **Asr** | Afternoon until sunset | The afternoon prayer. Its start time differs between the Standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) and Hanafi schools. |
| **Maghrib** | Sunset until twilight ends | The sunset prayer, performed immediately after the sun has set. |
| **Isha** | Night until dawn | The night prayer, performed after twilight has disappeared. |

The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said: "The time for Dhuhr prayer is when the sun passes the meridian and a man's shadow is the same length as his height, and it lasts until the time for Asr prayer." (Sahih Muslim)

## Calculation methods

Different Islamic organizations use slightly different angles to determine Fajr and Isha times. The calculator above lets you choose from the most widely used methods.

| Method | Fajr angle | Isha angle | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Muslim World League (MWL)** | 18 degrees | 17 degrees | Widely used across the world, especially in Europe and parts of Asia. |
| **Islamic Society of North America (ISNA)** | 15 degrees | 15 degrees | Developed for higher latitudes; commonly used in the United States and Canada. |
| **Egyptian General Authority** | 19.5 degrees | 17.5 degrees | Used in Egypt, Sudan, and parts of Africa. |
| **Umm al-Qura** | 18.5 degrees | 90 min after Maghrib | Used in Saudi Arabia. Isha is fixed at 90 minutes after Maghrib in standard form. |
| **University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi** | 18 degrees | 18 degrees | Used in Pakistan, India, and Bangladesh. |
| **Kemenag Indonesia** | 20 degrees | 18 degrees | Used by the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs. |

<Info>
  The angle refers to the sun's depression below the horizon. A smaller angle means earlier Fajr and later Isha. At extreme latitudes, some methods may produce times that are not practically usable.
</Info>

## Asr juristic settings

The start time of Asr prayer differs between two major schools of thought:

- **Standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali)**: Asr begins when the length of an object's shadow equals its height plus the shadow at noon (shadow factor = 1).
- **Hanafi**: Asr begins when the length of an object's shadow equals twice its height plus the shadow at noon (shadow factor = 2).

This means Hanafi Asr time is typically about an hour later than Standard Asr. Choose the setting that matches your school of jurisprudence or local convention.

## Friday Jumu'ah

Jumu'ah is the Friday congregational prayer that replaces Dhuhr for men who are able to attend. It consists of a sermon (khutbah) followed by two rak'ahs of prayer.

The time for Jumu'ah is the same as the time for Dhuhr prayer: from after the sun passes its zenith until the time for Asr begins. Most mosques schedule Jumu'ah between 12:00 and 14:00 local time, though the exact time varies by mosque and country.

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "The best day on which the sun has risen is Friday. On it Adam was created, on it he was admitted to Paradise, and on it he was expelled from it." (Sahih Muslim)

## High-latitude rules

At latitudes above approximately 50 degrees north or below 50 degrees south, the sun may not depress far enough below the horizon for Fajr or Isha to be clearly defined during summer months. In these cases, the calculator shows "--" for affected prayers.

Common approaches used in practice include:

- **Nearest day**: Use the times from the nearest day when all prayers were computable.
- **Nearest latitude**: Use the times from the nearest latitude where all prayers are defined.
- **Middle of the night**: Split the night into two halves; Fajr starts the second half.
- **One-seventh of the night**: Divide the night into seven parts.

If you live in a high-latitude region, consult your local Islamic authority for the convention they follow.

## Yearly prayer times

Prayer times shift throughout the year because they are tied to the sun's position, not the clock. In summer, days are longer so Fajr is earlier and Isha is later. In winter, the opposite occurs.

The calculator above lets you pick any date to see how times change. Try selecting different months to observe:

- **Summer solstice**: The longest day of the year, with the earliest Fajr and latest Isha.
- **Winter solstice**: The shortest day of the year, with the latest Fajr and earliest Isha.
- **Equinoxes**: Day and night are roughly equal; prayer times are most balanced.

If you need a full-year timetable for your city, many mosques and Islamic organizations publish annual prayer schedules. You can also use the calculator to check any specific date.

export const PrayerTimesCalculator = () => {
  const cities = [
    { name: "Makkah", lat: 21.4225, lng: 39.8262, offset: 3 },
    { name: "Madinah", lat: 24.4672, lng: 39.6111, offset: 3 },
    { name: "Jakarta", lat: -6.2088, lng: 106.8456, offset: 7 },
    { name: "Kuala Lumpur", lat: 3.139, lng: 101.6869, offset: 8 },
    { name: "Istanbul", lat: 41.0082, lng: 28.9784, offset: 3 },
    { name: "Cairo", lat: 30.0444, lng: 31.2357, offset: 2 },
    { name: "London", lat: 51.5074, lng: -0.1278, offset: 0 },
    { name: "New York", lat: 40.7128, lng: -74.006, offset: -5 },
    { name: "Karachi", lat: 24.8607, lng: 67.0011, offset: 5 },
    { name: "Dhaka", lat: 23.8103, lng: 90.4125, offset: 6 },
  ];

  const methods = [
    { name: "MWL", fajr: 18, isha: 17, label: "Muslim World League" },
    { name: "ISNA", fajr: 15, isha: 15, label: "ISNA (North America)" },
    { name: "Egyptian", fajr: 19.5, isha: 17.5, label: "Egyptian General Authority" },
    { name: "UmmAlQura", fajr: 18.5, isha: 90, label: "Umm al-Qura (Saudi Arabia)", fixedIsha: true },
    { name: "Karachi", fajr: 18, isha: 18, label: "University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi" },
    { name: "Kemenag", fajr: 20, isha: 18, label: "Kemenag Indonesia" },
  ];

  const [cityIndex, setCityIndex] = useState(0);
  const [custom, setCustom] = useState(false);
  const [lat, setLat] = useState(21.4225);
  const [lng, setLng] = useState(39.8262);
  const [offset, setOffset] = useState(3);
  const [dateStr, setDateStr] = useState(() => {
    const d = new Date();
    return d.toISOString().split("T")[0];
  });
  const [methodIndex, setMethodIndex] = useState(0);
  const [asrJuristic, setAsrJuristic] = useState(1); // 1 = Standard, 2 = Hanafi

  useEffect(() => {
    if (!custom) {
      const c = cities[cityIndex];
      setLat(c.lat);
      setLng(c.lng);
      setOffset(c.offset);
    }
  }, [cityIndex, custom]);

  function toRad(deg) { return (deg * Math.PI) / 180; }
  function toDeg(rad) { return (rad * 180) / Math.PI; }

  function julianDay(date) {
    const y = date.getFullYear();
    const m = date.getMonth() + 1;
    const d = date.getDate();
    let a = Math.floor((14 - m) / 12);
    let yy = y + 4800 - a;
    let mm = m + 12 * a - 3;
    let jd = d + Math.floor((153 * mm + 2) / 5) + 365 * yy + Math.floor(yy / 4) - Math.floor(yy / 100) + Math.floor(yy / 400) - 32045;
    return jd;
  }

  function sunDeclination(jd) {
    const d = jd - 2451545.0;
    const g = toRad((357.529 + 0.98560028 * d) % 360);
    const q = toRad((280.459 + 0.98564736 * d) % 360);
    const L = toRad((q + toDeg(g) + 1.914 * Math.sin(g) + 0.020 * Math.sin(2 * g)) % 360);
    const e = 23.439 - 0.00000036 * d;
    return Math.asin(Math.sin(e) * Math.sin(L));
  }

  function equationOfTime(jd) {
    const d = jd - 2451545.0;
    const g = toRad((357.529 + 0.98560028 * d) % 360);
    const q = toRad((280.459 + 0.98564736 * d) % 360);
    const L = toRad((q + toDeg(g) + 1.914 * Math.sin(g) + 0.020 * Math.sin(2 * g)) % 360);
    const e = toRad(23.439 - 0.00000036 * d);
    const RA = toDeg(Math.atan2(Math.cos(e) * Math.sin(L), Math.cos(L))) / 15;
    const EqT = q / toRad(15) - RA;
    return EqT;
  }

  function computeTimes() {
    const date = new Date(dateStr + "T12:00:00");
    const jd = julianDay(date);
    const decl = sunDeclination(jd);
    const eqt = equationOfTime(jd);
    const method = methods[methodIndex];

    const latRad = toRad(lat);
    const lngDeg = lng;

    // Dhuhr = midday - equation of time + longitude offset from UTC
    const dhuhrRaw = 12 + (4 * -lngDeg) / 60 - eqt;
    const dhuhr = dhuhrRaw + offset;

    // Sunrise / Sunset
    const sunriseAngle = toRad(0.833);
    const sunriseH = Math.acos((Math.cos(sunriseAngle) / (Math.cos(latRad) * Math.cos(decl))) - Math.tan(latRad) * Math.tan(decl));
    const sunriseRaw = dhuhrRaw - (toDeg(sunriseH) / 15);
    const sunsetRaw = dhuhrRaw + (toDeg(sunriseH) / 15);
    const sunrise = sunriseRaw + offset;
    const sunset = sunsetRaw + offset;

    // Fajr
    const fajrAngle = toRad(method.fajr);
    let fajrH = Math.acos((Math.cos(fajrAngle) / (Math.cos(latRad) * Math.cos(decl))) - Math.tan(latRad) * Math.tan(decl));
    if (isNaN(fajrH)) fajrH = null;
    const fajrRaw = fajrH !== null ? dhuhrRaw - (toDeg(fajrH) / 15) : null;
    const fajr = fajrRaw !== null ? fajrRaw + offset : null;

    // Maghrib
    const maghrib = sunset;

    // Isha
    let isha;
    if (method.fixedIsha) {
      isha = maghrib + method.isha / 60;
    } else {
      const ishaAngle = toRad(method.isha);
      let ishaH = Math.acos((Math.cos(ishaAngle) / (Math.cos(latRad) * Math.cos(decl))) - Math.tan(latRad) * Math.tan(decl));
      if (isNaN(ishaH)) ishaH = null;
      const ishaRaw = ishaH !== null ? dhuhrRaw + (toDeg(ishaH) / 15) : null;
      isha = ishaRaw !== null ? ishaRaw + offset : null;
    }

    // Asr
    const asrFactor = asrJuristic;
    const asrAngle = Math.atan(1 / (asrFactor + Math.tan(Math.abs(latRad - decl))));
    let asrH = Math.acos((Math.sin(asrAngle) / (Math.cos(latRad) * Math.cos(decl))) - Math.tan(latRad) * Math.tan(decl));
    if (isNaN(asrH)) asrH = null;
    const asrRaw = asrH !== null ? dhuhrRaw + (toDeg(asrH) / 15) : null;
    const asr = asrRaw !== null ? asrRaw + offset : null;

    return { fajr, sunrise, dhuhr, asr, maghrib, isha };
  }

  function fmt(t) {
    if (t === null || isNaN(t)) return "--";
    let h = Math.floor(t);
    let m = Math.round((t - h) * 60);
    if (m === 60) { m = 0; h += 1; }
    if (h >= 24) h -= 24;
    const hh = h.toString().padStart(2, "0");
    const mm = m.toString().padStart(2, "0");
    return `${hh}:${mm}`;
  }

  const times = computeTimes();

  const now = new Date();
  const todayStr = now.toISOString().split("T")[0];
  const isToday = dateStr === todayStr;

  const prayers = [
    { key: "fajr", label: "Fajr" },
    { key: "sunrise", label: "Sunrise" },
    { key: "dhuhr", label: "Dhuhr" },
    { key: "asr", label: "Asr" },
    { key: "maghrib", label: "Maghrib" },
    { key: "isha", label: "Isha" },
  ];

  let nextPrayer = null;
  if (isToday) {
    const currentMinutes = now.getHours() + now.getMinutes() / 60;
    for (const p of prayers) {
      const t = times[p.key];
      if (t !== null && !isNaN(t) && t > currentMinutes) {
        nextPrayer = p.key;
        break;
      }
    }
    if (!nextPrayer && times.fajr !== null && !isNaN(times.fajr)) {
      nextPrayer = "fajr";
    }
  }

  const inputStyle = {
    padding: "8px 10px",
    borderRadius: 8,
    border: "1px solid rgba(127,127,127,.35)",
    background: "transparent",
    color: "inherit",
    fontSize: 14,
    width: "100%",
  };

  const labelStyle = {
    display: "flex",
    flexDirection: "column",
    gap: 4,
    fontSize: 13,
    fontWeight: 500,
  };

  const rowStyle = {
    display: "flex",
    alignItems: "center",
    justifyContent: "space-between",
    padding: "10px 12px",
    borderRadius: 8,
    fontSize: 15,
  };

  return (
    <div className="not-prose" style={{ border: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.25)", borderRadius: 12, padding: 16, background: "rgba(15,118,110,0.04)" }}>
      <div style={{ display: "grid", gridTemplateColumns: "repeat(auto-fit, minmax(220px, 1fr))", gap: 12, marginBottom: 16 }}>
        <label style={labelStyle}>
          City preset
          <select style={inputStyle} value={custom ? "custom" : cityIndex} onChange={(e) => {
            const val = e.target.value;
            if (val === "custom") {
              setCustom(true);
            } else {
              setCustom(false);
              setCityIndex(Number(val));
            }
          }}>
            {cities.map((c, i) => (
              <option key={c.name} value={i}>{c.name}</option>
            ))}
            <option value="custom">Custom location</option>
          </select>
        </label>

        {custom && (
          <>
            <label style={labelStyle}>
              Latitude
              <input type="number" step="0.0001" style={inputStyle} value={lat} onChange={(e) => setLat(Number(e.target.value))} />
            </label>
            <label style={labelStyle}>
              Longitude
              <input type="number" step="0.0001" style={inputStyle} value={lng} onChange={(e) => setLng(Number(e.target.value))} />
            </label>
          </>
        )}

        <label style={labelStyle}>
          UTC offset (hours)
          <input type="number" step="0.5" min="-12" max="14" style={inputStyle} value={offset} onChange={(e) => setOffset(Number(e.target.value))} />
        </label>

        <label style={labelStyle}>
          Date
          <input type="date" style={inputStyle} value={dateStr} onChange={(e) => setDateStr(e.target.value)} />
        </label>

        <label style={labelStyle}>
          Calculation method
          <select style={inputStyle} value={methodIndex} onChange={(e) => setMethodIndex(Number(e.target.value))}>
            {methods.map((m, i) => (
              <option key={m.name} value={i}>{m.label}</option>
            ))}
          </select>
        </label>

        <label style={labelStyle}>
          Asr juristic method
          <select style={inputStyle} value={asrJuristic} onChange={(e) => setAsrJuristic(Number(e.target.value))}>
            <option value={1}>Standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali)</option>
            <option value={2}>Hanafi</option>
          </select>
        </label>
      </div>

      <div style={{ border: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)", borderRadius: 10, overflow: "hidden", background: "rgba(15,118,110,0.06)" }}>
        {prayers.map((p) => {
          const t = times[p.key];
          const isNext = nextPrayer === p.key;
          return (
            <div key={p.key} style={{
              ...rowStyle,
              background: isNext ? "rgba(212,160,23,0.12)" : "transparent",
              borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.1)",
            }}>
              <span style={{ fontWeight: 600, display: "flex", alignItems: "center", gap: 8 }}>
                {isNext && <span style={{ width: 8, height: 8, borderRadius: "50%", background: "#D4A017", display: "inline-block" }} />}
                {p.label}
              </span>
              <span style={{ fontFamily: "monospace", fontSize: 16, fontWeight: 500 }}>{fmt(t)}</span>
            </div>
          );
        })}
      </div>

      {isToday && nextPrayer && (
        <div style={{ marginTop: 10, fontSize: 13, color: "#0F766E", fontWeight: 500 }}>
          Next prayer: {prayers.find(p => p.key === nextPrayer)?.label} at {fmt(times[nextPrayer])}
        </div>
      )}
    </div>
  );
};

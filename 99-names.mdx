The 99 Names of Allah, known as Asma ul Husna (the Most Beautiful Names), are attributes through which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the teachings of the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him. These names invite believers to know their Creator more deeply and to call upon Him by names that reflect His infinite perfection, mercy, and power.

Allah says in the Quran:

> "And to Allah belong the most beautiful names, so invoke Him by them. And leave those who practice deviation concerning His names. They will be recompensed for what they have been doing." (Quran 7:180)

The Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, said: "Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one. Whoever enumerates them will enter Paradise." (Sahih al-Bukhari 2736, Sahih Muslim 2677)

## Virtues and Etiquette of Remembering Allah's Names

### Memorization and Reflection

The Prophet, peace be upon him, specifically linked the enumeration of these names to Paradise. Scholars have differed on whether this means memorizing them, understanding their meanings, or calling upon Allah through them in supplication. The strongest view combines all three: knowing the names, comprehending their meanings, and using them in dua.

### Calling Upon Allah by His Names

Allah invites His servants to call upon Him by His beautiful names:

> "Say, Call upon Allah or call upon the Most Gracious. Whichever name you call, to Him belong the most beautiful names." (Quran 17:110)

### Dhikr Practices

Many Muslims incorporate the names into daily dhikr, reciting them individually or in gatherings. Some recite all ninety-nine names after each obligatory prayer, while others focus on one name at a time for deeper contemplation.

## Scholarly Notes

Scholars of Islamic theology (aqeedah) emphasize that these names are attributes of Allah's essence and actions, not separate from Him. They describe what Allah is and does, while affirming that nothing is like unto Him. The names are all perfect and free from any deficiency.

Different schools of thought may vary slightly in the exact ordering or transliteration of certain names, but the core ninety-nine are agreed upon by the vast majority of scholars. Some names, such as Al-Muqsit (The Equitable) and Al-Muntaqim (The Avenger), appear in some lists and not others depending on the chain of narration followed.

For a deeper exploration of the meanings and spiritual significance of these names, see our detailed article.

    setRandomName(names[idx]);
  };

  return (
    <div className="not-prose" style={{ border: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.3)", borderRadius: 12, padding: 16, background: "rgba(15,118,110,0.06)" }}>
      <div style={{ display: "flex", gap: 8, flexWrap: "wrap", marginBottom: 12 }}>
        <input
          type="text"
          placeholder="Search by name or meaning..."
          value={query}
          onChange={(e) => setQuery(e.target.value)}
          style={{
            flex: 1,
            minWidth: 200,
            padding: "8px 12px",
            borderRadius: 8,
            border: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.35)",
            background: "transparent",
            color: "inherit",
            font: "inherit",
          }}
        />
        <button
          type="button"
          onClick={pickRandom}
          style={{
            padding: "8px 14px",
            borderRadius: 8,
            border: "none",
            background: "#0F766E",
            color: "#fff",
            font: "inherit",
            cursor: "pointer",
          }}
        >
          Name of the Day
        </button>
      </div>

      {randomName && (
        <div
          style={{
            marginBottom: 12,
            padding: 12,
            borderRadius: 8,
            background: "rgba(212,160,23,0.12)",
            border: "1px solid rgba(212,160,23,0.35)",
          }}
        >
          <div style={{ fontSize: 12, opacity: 0.7, marginBottom: 4 }}>Name of the Day (#{randomName.num})</div>
          <div style={{ fontSize: 22, fontWeight: 700, color: "#0F766E" }}>{randomName.arabic}</div>
          <div style={{ fontSize: 16, fontWeight: 600 }}>{randomName.translit}</div>
          <div style={{ fontSize: 14, opacity: 0.9 }}>{randomName.meaning}</div>
        </div>
      )}

      <div
        style={{
          maxHeight: 320,
          overflowY: "auto",
          border: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)",
          borderRadius: 8,
          background: "rgba(255,255,255,0.5)",
        }}
      >
        {filtered.length === 0 ? (
          <div style={{ padding: 16, textAlign: "center", opacity: 0.7 }}>No names match your search.</div>
        ) : (
          <table style={{ width: "100%", borderCollapse: "collapse", fontSize: 14 }}>
            <thead>
              <tr style={{ background: "rgba(15,118,110,0.1)" }}>
                <th style={{ padding: "8px 10px", textAlign: "left", borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)", width: 50 }}>#</th>
                <th style={{ padding: "8px 10px", textAlign: "left", borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)" }}>Arabic</th>
                <th style={{ padding: "8px 10px", textAlign: "left", borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)" }}>Transliteration</th>
                <th style={{ padding: "8px 10px", textAlign: "left", borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)" }}>Meaning</th>
              </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
              {filtered.map((n) => (
                <tr key={n.num} style={{ borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.1)" }}>
                  <td style={{ padding: "6px 10px" }}>{n.num}</td>
                  <td style={{ padding: "6px 10px", fontWeight: 600, color: "#0F766E" }}>{n.arabic}</td>
                  <td style={{ padding: "6px 10px" }}>{n.translit}</td>
                  <td style={{ padding: "6px 10px" }}>{n.meaning}</td>
                </tr>
              ))}
            </tbody>
          </table>
        )}
      </div>
      <div style={{ fontSize: 12, opacity: 0.6, marginTop: 8 }}>Showing {filtered.length} of {names.length} names</div>
    </div>
  );
};

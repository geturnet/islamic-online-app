Islamic Online is a free digital resource created to help Muslims and learners around the world access authentic Islamic knowledge and practical tools with ease, clarity, and respect.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to make the Quran, Hadith, Dua, prayer times, and core Islamic teachings accessible to everyone, regardless of location or background. We believe that reliable religious information should be easy to find, clearly presented, and grounded in authentic sources.

## What We Offer

- **Quran and Hadith**: Read and reflect on the Book of Allah and the authentic sayings of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).
- **Prayer Times and Calendar**: Accurate Salah times and Hijri dates to help you organize your worship.
- **Islamic Tools**: Qibla direction, date conversion, Zakat calculation, and a digital tasbih counter.
- **Articles and Guides**: Trusted articles on the Five Pillars, Salah, Ramadan, Zakat, and the Names of Allah.
- **Daily Supplications**: A collection of Duas for every occasion, drawn from the Quran and Sunnah.

## Editorial Approach

We take accuracy seriously. Our content is based on the Quran, Sahih Hadith collections (such as Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim), and established scholarly works. References are provided wherever possible so readers can verify and explore further.

Where scholars hold different views, such as on prayer calculation methods or madhhab rulings, we present the differences briefly and fairly without favoring one position over another in a way that excludes valid opinions.

We do not issue fatwas. For personal religious rulings, we encourage readers to consult qualified local scholars.

## Corrections and Feedback

We welcome corrections and suggestions. If you notice an error in a reference, translation, or explanation, please contact us through our [Contact page](/contact). We review all feedback and update content when needed.

The Islamic calendar, also known as the Hijri calendar, is a lunar calendar used by Muslims worldwide to determine the dates of religious observances such as Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, and Eid al-Adha. It consists of 12 months in a year of 354 or 355 days, making it about 11 days shorter than the Gregorian solar year.

## The Hijri calendar explained

The Islamic calendar began in 622 CE, the year of the Hijrah (migration) of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and his companions from Makkah to Madinah. This event marks the beginning of the Islamic era.

Because the calendar is purely lunar, each month begins with the sighting of the new crescent moon (hilal). A lunar month is approximately 29.53 days, so Islamic months alternate between 29 and 30 days. Over 12 months, this adds up to about 354 days, causing the Hijri year to drift backward through the Gregorian calendar by roughly 11 days each year.

Allah says in the Quran:

<p className="io-arabic">
  إِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُورِ عِندَ اللَّهِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ يَوْمَ خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ

"Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve months in the register of Allah since He created the heavens and the earth." (Quran 9:36)

## Sacred months

Four months in the Hijri calendar are designated as sacred (al-Ashhur al-Hurum), during which warfare was traditionally prohibited and certain religious observances are emphasized.

Allah says in the Quran:

<p className="io-arabic">
  إِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُورِ عِندَ اللَّهِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ يَوْمَ خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ مِنْهَا أَرْبَعَةٌ حُرُمٌ
</p>

"Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve months in the register of Allah since He created the heavens and the earth, of which four are sacred." (Quran 9:36)

The four sacred months are:

1. **Muharram** (1st month)
2. **Rajab** (7th month)
3. **Dhul-Qa'dah** (11th month)
4. **Dhul-Hijjah** (12th month)

## Key dates in the Islamic calendar

Here are the most important dates and observances in the Hijri year. Note that exact Gregorian dates shift earlier by about 11 days each year because the Hijri year is shorter.

| Date | Event | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 Muharram** | Islamic New Year | Marks the beginning of the new Hijri year and the Hijrah. |
| **10 Muharram** | Day of Ashura | Fasting is recommended. Commemorates the martyrdom of Imam Hussein, the grandson of the Prophet, at Karbala. |
| **12 Rabi al-Awwal** | Mawlid al-Nabi | The birthday of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Observed by many Muslims worldwide, though practices vary. |
| **27 Rajab** | Isra and Mi'raj | The Night Journey and Ascension of the Prophet. Commonly observed on this date, though some scholars differ on the exact date. |
| **1 Ramadan** | Start of Ramadan | The beginning of the month of fasting. Determined by moon sighting. |
| **Last ten nights of Ramadan** | Laylat al-Qadr | The Night of Decree, better than a thousand months. Most scholars emphasize the odd nights of the last ten, especially the 27th. |
| **1 Shawwal** | Eid al-Fitr | The festival of breaking the fast, celebrated after Ramadan ends. |
| **9 Dhul-Hijjah** | Day of Arafah | The day pilgrims stand on Mount Arafat. Fasting is highly recommended for non-pilgrims. |
| **10 Dhul-Hijjah** | Eid al-Adha | The festival of sacrifice, commemorating the willingness of Ibrahim (Abraham) to sacrifice his son. |

<Info>
  Because the Islamic calendar is lunar, the exact Gregorian dates of these events change every year. Local moon sighting may also cause dates to differ by one day from country to country.

## Ramadan and the lunar cycle

Ramadan is the ninth month of the Hijri calendar and the holiest month for Muslims. It is the month in which the Quran was first revealed, as mentioned in Surah al-Baqarah:

<p className="io-arabic">
  شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِي أُنزِلَ فِيهِ الْقُرْآنُ هُدًى لِّلنَّاسِ وَبَيِّنَاتٍ مِّنَ الْهُدَىٰ وَالْفُرْقَانِ
</p>

"The month of Ramadan is that in which the Quran was revealed as guidance for mankind, with clear proofs of guidance and the criterion." (Quran 2:185)

Because the Hijri year is about 11 days shorter than the Gregorian year, Ramadan moves backward through the seasons over a 33-year cycle. Muslims may experience Ramadan in summer, winter, spring, or autumn depending on the year.

## Moon sighting vs. calculation

There are two main approaches to determining the start of an Islamic month:

- **Moon sighting (rukyah)**: The traditional method, where the new crescent moon is observed visually after sunset on the 29th day of the month. If the moon is sighted, the new month begins; otherwise, the current month completes 30 days.
- **Astronomical calculation**: Using precise calculations to predict when the moon will be visible. Some countries and organizations use this method to plan ahead.

The difference between these methods, combined with geographic variation in moon visibility, can cause the same Hijri date to be observed on different Gregorian dates in different parts of the world.

export const HijriToday = () => {
  const [today, setToday] = useState(() => new Date());

  useEffect(() => {
    const id = setInterval(() => setToday(new Date()), 60000);
    return () => clearInterval(id);
  }, []);

  // Tabular (Kuwaiti) algorithm: approximate Hijri date
  function gregorianToHijri(date) {
    const d = date.getDate();
    const m = date.getMonth() + 1;
    const y = date.getFullYear();

    let jd;
    if (y > 1582 || (y === 1582 && m > 10) || (y === 1582 && m === 10 && d >= 15)) {
      const a = Math.floor((14 - m) / 12);
      const yy = y + 4800 - a;
      const mm = m + 12 * a - 3;
      jd = d + Math.floor((153 * mm + 2) / 5) + 365 * yy + Math.floor(yy / 4) - Math.floor(yy / 100) + Math.floor(yy / 400) - 32045;
    } else {
      const a = Math.floor((14 - m) / 12);
      const yy = y + 4800 - a;
      const mm = m + 12 * a - 3;
      jd = d + Math.floor((153 * mm + 2) / 5) + 365 * yy + Math.floor(yy / 4) - 32083;
    }

    const l = jd - 1948440 + 10632;
    const n = Math.floor((l - 1) / 10631);
    const r = l - 10631 * n + 354;
    const j = (Math.floor((10985 - r) / 5316)) * (Math.floor((50 * r) / 17719)) + (Math.floor(r / 5670)) * (Math.floor((43 * r) / 15238));
    const r1 = r - (Math.floor((30 - j) / 15)) * (Math.floor((17719 * j) / 50)) - (Math.floor(j / 16)) * (Math.floor((15238 * j) / 43)) + 29;
    const hMonth = Math.floor((24 * r1) / 709);
    const hDay = r1 - Math.floor((709 * hMonth) / 24);
    const hYear = 30 * n + j - 30;

    return { day: hDay, month: hMonth, year: hYear };
  }

  function hijriMonthName(m) {
    const names = [
      "Muharram", "Safar", "Rabi al-Awwal", "Rabi al-Thani",
      "Jumada al-Awwal", "Jumada al-Thani", "Rajab", "Sha'ban",
      "Ramadan", "Shawwal", "Dhul-Qa'dah", "Dhul-Hijjah"
    ];
    return names[m - 1] || "";
  }

  function hijriMonthDays(hYear, hMonth) {
    // Tabular: odd months have 30 days, even have 29, except month 12 in leap years
    const isLeap = [2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26, 29].includes(hYear % 30);
    if (hMonth === 12 && isLeap) return 30;
    return hMonth % 2 === 1 ? 30 : 29;
  }

  function hijriToGregorian(hYear, hMonth, hDay) {
    const jd = Math.floor((11 * hYear + 3) / 30) + 354 * hYear + 30 * hMonth - Math.floor((hMonth - 1) / 2) + hDay + 1948440 - 385;
    const l = jd + 68569;
    const n = Math.floor((4 * l) / 146097);
    const l2 = l - Math.floor((146097 * n + 3) / 4);
    const i = Math.floor((4000 * (l2 + 1)) / 1461001);
    const l3 = l2 - Math.floor((1461 * i) / 4) + 31;
    const j = Math.floor((80 * l3) / 2447);
    const d = l3 - Math.floor((2447 * j) / 80);
    const l4 = Math.floor(j / 11);
    const m = j + 2 - 12 * l4;
    const y = 100 * (n - 49) + i + l4;
    return new Date(y, m - 1, d);
  }

  const hijri = gregorianToHijri(today);
  const monthDays = hijriMonthDays(hijri.year, hijri.month);

  const weekdayNames = ["Sun", "Mon", "Tue", "Wed", "Thu", "Fri", "Sat"];
  const monthName = hijriMonthName(hijri.month);

  // Build grid: find Gregorian date of Hijri day 1 of current month
  const firstGreg = hijriToGregorian(hijri.year, hijri.month, 1);
  const startWeekday = firstGreg.getDay();

  const cells = [];
  for (let i = 0; i < startWeekday; i++) {
    cells.push({ type: "empty" });
  }
  for (let d = 1; d <= monthDays; d++) {
    const g = hijriToGregorian(hijri.year, hijri.month, d);
    cells.push({
      type: "day",
      hDay: d,
      gDate: g,
      isToday: g.toDateString() === today.toDateString(),
    });
  }

  const headerStyle = {
    background: "rgba(15,118,110,0.08)",
    color: "inherit",
    padding: "8px 4px",
    textAlign: "center",
    fontSize: 12,
    fontWeight: 600,
    borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)",
  };

  const cellStyle = {
    padding: "10px 4px",
    textAlign: "center",
    fontSize: 13,
    borderBottom: "1px solid rgba(127,127,127,.15)",
    borderRight: "1px solid rgba(127,127,127,.15)",
  };

  return (
    <div className="not-prose" style={{ border: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.25)", borderRadius: 12, padding: 16, background: "rgba(15,118,110,0.04)" }}>
      <div style={{ textAlign: "center", marginBottom: 12 }}>
        <div style={{ fontSize: 18, fontWeight: 700, color: "#0F766E" }}>
          {hijri.day} {monthName} {hijri.year} AH
        </div>
        <div style={{ fontSize: 13, opacity: 0.7, marginTop: 4 }}>
          Approximate Hijri date for {today.toLocaleDateString(undefined, { weekday: "long", year: "numeric", month: "long", day: "numeric" })}
        </div>
      </div>

      <div style={{ display: "grid", gridTemplateColumns: "repeat(7, 1fr)", border: "1px solid rgba(15,118,110,0.2)", borderRadius: 8, overflow: "hidden" }}>
        {weekdayNames.map((w) => (
          <div key={w} style={headerStyle}>{w}</div>
        ))}
        {cells.map((c, i) => {
          if (c.type === "empty") {
            return <div key={i} style={cellStyle} />;
          }
          const gStr = c.gDate.toLocaleDateString(undefined, { month: "short", day: "numeric" });
          return (
            <div key={i} style={{
              ...cellStyle,
              background: c.isToday ? "rgba(212,160,23,0.15)" : "transparent",
              fontWeight: c.isToday ? 700 : 400,
            }}>
              <div>{c.hDay}</div>
              <div style={{ fontSize: 10, opacity: 0.6 }}>{gStr}</div>
            </div>
          );
        })}
      </div>

      <div style={{ marginTop: 10, fontSize: 12, opacity: 0.7, textAlign: "center" }}>
        Calendar shows the current Hijri month with corresponding Gregorian dates.
      </div>
    </div>
  );
};

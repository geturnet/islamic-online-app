Dua is the essence of worship and the most powerful means by which a servant communicates with Allah. The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said that dua is the very purpose of worship and that Allah is near, responding to the call of every caller when they call upon Him. This page gathers authentic supplications from the Quran and authentic Sunnah for daily occasions, organized by category for easy reference.

## Morning and Evening Duas

### Upon Waking Up

When waking from sleep, the Prophet (peace be upon him) would wipe his face with his hands and say:

<p className="io-arabic">
  الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahil-lathee ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushoor.

**Meaning:** All praise is due to Allah, who has given us life after He caused us to die, and to Him is the resurrection.

_(Sahih al-Bukhari 6312, Sahih Muslim 2711)_

---

### Before Sleeping

The Prophet (peace be upon him) would recite the last three surahs (al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas) and blow into his hands, then wipe over his body for protection. He would also say:

<p className="io-arabic">
  بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا
</p>

**Transliteration:** Bismika Allahumma amootu wa ahya.

**Meaning:** In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.

_(Sahih al-Bukhari 6314, Sahih Muslim 2712)_

Another supplication before sleep:

<p className="io-arabic">
  اللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahumma qinee 'adhabaka yawma tab'athu 'ibadak.

**Meaning:** O Allah, protect me from Your punishment on the Day You resurrect Your servants.

_(Sunan Abu Dawud 5045, authenticated by scholars)_

## Eating and Drinking

### Before Eating

<p className="io-arabic">
  بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Bismillah.

**Meaning:** In the name of Allah.

_(Sahih al-Bukhari 5376, Sahih Muslim 2017)_

If one forgets to say it at the beginning, they should say when they remember:

<p className="io-arabic">
  بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirah.

**Meaning:** In the name of Allah, at its beginning and at its end.

_(Sunan at-Tirmidhi 1858, authenticated by scholars)_

---

### After Eating

<p className="io-arabic">
  الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahil-lathee at'amani hatha wa razaqaneehi min ghayri hawlin minnee wa la quwwah.

**Meaning:** All praise is due to Allah, who has fed me this and provided it for me without any might or power from me.

_(Sunan Abu Dawud 4023, authenticated by scholars)_

---

### When Breaking Fast (Iftar)

<p className="io-arabic">
  ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Dhahaba adh-dhama'u wabtallatil-'urooqu wa thabatal-ajru in sha' Allah.

**Meaning:** The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills.

_(Sunan Abu Dawud 3857, authenticated by scholars)_

## Entering and Leaving Home

### Upon Entering Home

<p className="io-arabic">
  اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ الْمَوْلِجِ وَخَيْرَ الْمَخْرَجِ بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا وَبِسْمِ اللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا وَعَلَى اللَّهِ رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا
</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahumma innee as'aluka khayral-mawliji wa khayral-makhraji. Bismillahi walajna wa bismillahi kharajna wa 'alallahi rabbina tawakkalna.

**Meaning:** O Allah, I ask You for the best entry and the best exit. In the name of Allah we enter, and in the name of Allah we exit, and upon Allah, our Lord, we rely.

_(Sunan Abu Dawud 5096, authenticated by scholars)_

---

### Upon Leaving Home

<p className="io-arabic">
  بِسْمِ اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'alallahi wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

**Meaning:** In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might and no power except with Allah.

_(Sunan at-Tirmidhi 3518, authenticated by scholars)_

## Mosque Duas

### Upon Entering the Mosque

<p className="io-arabic">
  اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahumma iftah lee abwaba rahmatik.

**Meaning:** O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.

_(Sahih Muslim 713)_

---

### Upon Leaving the Mosque

<p className="io-arabic">
  اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahumma innee as'aluka min fadlik.

**Meaning:** O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.

_(Sahih Muslim 714)_

## Travel Duas

### When Beginning a Journey

<p className="io-arabic">
  اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَى رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ فِي سَفَرِنَا هَذَا الْبِرَّ وَالتَّقْوَى وَمِنَ الْعَمَلِ مَا تَرْضَى اللَّهُمَّ هَوِّنْ عَلَيْنَا سَفَرَنَا هَذَا وَاطْوِ عَنَّا بُعْدَهُ اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الصَّاحِبُ فِي السَّفَرِ وَالْخَلِيفَةُ فِي الْأَهْلِ اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ وَعْثَاءِ السَّفَرِ وَكَآبَةِ الْمَنْظَرِ وَسُوءِ الْمُنْقَلَبِ فِي الْمَالِ وَالْأَهْلِ وَالْوَلَدِ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar. Subhanal-lathee sakhkhara lana hatha wa ma kunna lahu muqrineen. Wa inna ila rabbina lamunqaliboon. Allahumma inna nas'aluka fee safarina hatha al-birra wat-taqwa, wa minal-'amali ma tardha. Allahumma hawwin 'alayna safarana hatha wa atwi 'anna bu'dah. Allahumma antas-sahibu fis-safari wal-khaleefatu fil-ahli. Allahumma innee a'udhu bika min wa'tha-is-safari, wa kaabatil-mandhari, wa soo'il-munqalabi fil-mali wal-ahli wal-walad.

**Meaning:** Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest. Glory be to Him who has subjected this to us, and we could not have done it ourselves. And indeed, to our Lord we will return. O Allah, we ask You on this journey for righteousness and taqwa, and for deeds that please You. O Allah, make this journey easy for us and fold up its distance for us. O Allah, You are the Companion on the journey and the Guardian over the family. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the hardships of travel, from the grief of returning, and from the evil state of affairs in wealth, family, and children.

_(Sahih Muslim 1342)_

---

### Upon Returning from Travel

<p className="io-arabic">
  آيِبُونَ تَائِبُونَ عَابِدُونَ لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Aa'iboona, ta'iboona, 'abidoona, li-rabbina hamidoon.

**Meaning:** We are returning, repenting, worshipping, and praising our Lord.

_(Sahih Muslim 1343)_

## Personal and Spiritual Duas

### Before Entering the Bathroom

<p className="io-arabic">
  اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخُبُثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahumma innee a'udhu bika minal-khubuthi wal-khabaa'ith.

**Meaning:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the male and female devils.

_(Sahih al-Bukhari 142, Sahih Muslim 375)_

---

### For Parents

Allah commands in the Quran:

<p className="io-arabic">
  رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا
</p>

**Transliteration:** Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayanee sagheera.

**Meaning:** My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small.

_(Surah al-Isra 17:24)_

---

### The Dua of Ibrahim for Makkah

<p className="io-arabic">
  رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar.

**Meaning:** Our Lord, give us in this world good and in the Hereafter good, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

_(Surah al-Baqarah 2:201)_

This is one of the most comprehensive and frequently recited supplications, covering both worldly and spiritual well-being.

---

### Seeking Knowledge

<p className="io-arabic">
  رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
</p>

**Transliteration:** Rabbi zidnee 'ilma.

**Meaning:** My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

_(Surah Ta-Ha 20:114)_

---

### Seeking Forgiveness (Sayyid al-Istighfar)

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that whoever says this supplication with firm faith in the morning and dies before evening will enter Paradise, and whoever says it in the evening and dies before morning will enter Paradise.

<p className="io-arabic">
  اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي فَاغْفِرْ لِي فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahumma anta rabbi la ilaha illa anta, khalaqtanee wa ana 'abduka, wa ana 'ala 'ahdika wa wa'dika mastata'tu. A'udhu bika min sharri ma sana'tu, aboo'u laka bi ni'matika 'alayya wa aboo'u laka bi dhanbee, faghfir lee fa-innahu la yaghfirudh-dhunooba illa anta.

**Meaning:** O Allah, You are my Lord. There is no deity worthy of worship except You. You created me, and I am Your servant, and I am faithful to my covenant and promise to You to the best of my ability. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge before You Your blessings upon me, and I acknowledge before You my sin, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.

_(Sahih al-Bukhari 6306, Sahih Muslim 2705)_

---

### In Times of Distress and Anxiety

<p className="io-arabic">
  لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ
</p>

**Transliteration:** La ilaha illa anta subhanaka innee kuntu minadh-dhalimeen.

**Meaning:** There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.

_(Surah al-Anbiya 21:87)_

This is the supplication of Yunus (Jonah) when he was in the belly of the whale. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that no Muslim ever supplicates with it for anything except that Allah responds.

---

### After Finishing Prayer

<p className="io-arabic">
  أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah, astaghfirullah. Allahumma antas-salamu wa minkas-salamu, tabarakta ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram.

**Meaning:** I seek forgiveness from Allah (three times). O Allah, You are Peace, and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Possessor of majesty and honor.

_(Sahih Muslim 591)_

---

### The Dua of Prophet Zakariya

<p className="io-arabic">
  رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِنْ لَدُنْكَ ذُرِّيَّةً طَيِّبَةً إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ الدُّعَاءِ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Rabbi hab lee min ladunka dhurriyyatan tayyibatan innaka samee'ud-du'a.

**Meaning:** My Lord, grant me from Yourself a good offspring. Indeed, You are the Hearer of supplication.

_(Surah Ali 'Imran 3:38)_

---

### When Facing Difficulty

<p className="io-arabic">
  حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal-wakeel.

**Meaning:** Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.

_(Surah Ali 'Imran 3:173)_

This was the statement of Ibrahim when he was thrown into the fire, and the statement of the Prophet (peace be upon him) and his Companions when they were told that their enemies had gathered against them.

---

### For Guidance and Steadfastness

<p className="io-arabic">
  رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْوَهَّابُ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Rabbana la tuzigh quloobana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmatan innaka antal-wahhab.

**Meaning:** Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us from Yourself mercy. Indeed, You are the Bestower.

_(Surah Ali 'Imran 3:8)_

---

### When Overwhelmed by Gratitude

<p className="io-arabic">
  الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
</p>

**Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahi Rabbil-'aalameen.

**Meaning:** All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds.

_(Surah al-Fatihah 1:2)_

This phrase opens the Quran and is the most frequently recited expression of gratitude. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that alhamdulillah fills the scale on the Day of Judgment.

<Tip>
  Memorize a few core duas deeply rather than learning many superficially. The Prophet (peace be upon him) consistently used the same supplications, demonstrating that sincerity and presence of heart matter more than the length or complexity of the words.
</Tip>

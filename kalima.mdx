The Six Kalimas are foundational declarations taught across the Muslim world, particularly in South Asia, to help believers memorize core statements of faith, praise, repentance, and rejection of disbelief. While the Shahadah (the testimony of faith) is the only Kalima required for entering Islam, all six serve as important spiritual anchors for daily reflection and teaching.

## First Kalima: Tayyib (The Word of Purity)

The Kalima Tayyib affirms the Oneness of Allah and the prophethood of Muhammad, peace be upon him. It is the essence of the Islamic declaration of faith.

<p className="io-arabic">لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ</p>

**Transliteration:** La ilaha illallah, Muhammadur Rasulullah.

**English Meaning:** There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.

This is the Shahadah, the testimony by which a person enters Islam. Allah says in the Quran:

> "Allah witnesses that there is no deity except Him, and so do the angels and those of knowledge, that He is maintaining creation in justice. There is no deity except Him, the Exalted in Might, the Wise." (Quran 3:18)

## Second Kalima: Shahadat (The Testimony)

The Kalima Shahadat expands on the first by explicitly bearing witness to the oneness of Allah and the messengership of Muhammad.

<p className="io-arabic">أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ</p>

**Transliteration:** Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah wahdahu la sharika lahu, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluhu.

**English Meaning:** I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, He is One, there is no partner to Him, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger.

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Whoever says La ilaha illallah sincerely from his heart will enter Paradise." (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)

## Third Kalima: Tamjeed (The Word of Glorification)

The Kalima Tamjeed glorifies Allah and affirms His absolute greatness, power, and majesty.

<p className="io-arabic">سُبْحَانَ ٱللَّٰهِ وَٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّٰهِ وَلَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَٱللَّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّٰهِ ٱلْعَلِيِّ ٱلْعَظِيمِ</p>

**Transliteration:** Subhanallahi walhamdulillahi wa la ilaha illallahu wallahu akbar, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil aliyyil azim.

**English Meaning:** Glory be to Allah, and all praise be to Allah, and there is no god but Allah, and Allah is the Greatest. And there is no power and no strength except with Allah, the Exalted, the Mighty.

This kalima combines the four phrases of tasbih that the Prophet, peace be upon him, taught as the most beloved words to Allah.

## Fourth Kalima: Tawheed (The Word of Oneness)

The Kalima Tawheed affirms the absolute oneness of Allah, free from any partner, son, or equal.

<p className="io-arabic">لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ ٱلْحَمْدُ يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ، وَهُوَ حَيٌّ لَا يَمُوتُ بِيَدِهِ ٱلْخَيْرُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ</p>

**Transliteration:** La ilaha illallah wahdahu la sharika lahu, lahul mulku wa lahul hamdu yuhyi wa yumitu, wa huwa hayyun la yamutu biyadihil khayru wa huwa ala kulli shayin qadir.

**English Meaning:** There is no god but Allah, He is One, there is no partner to Him. His is the dominion and His is the praise. He gives life and causes death, and He is Ever-Living and does not die. In His hand is all good, and He has power over all things.

This reflects the Quranic description of Allah as the Ever-Living, the Self-Subsisting:

> "Allah, there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Self-Sustaining. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth." (Quran 2:255)

## Fifth Kalima: Astaghfar (The Word of Penitence)

The Kalima Astaghfar is a prayer seeking forgiveness from Allah, acknowledging His mercy and power over all things.

<p className="io-arabic">أَسْتَغْفِرُ ٱللَّٰهَ رَبِّي مِنْ كُلِّ ذَنْبٍ أَذْنَبْتُهُ عَمَدًا أَوْ خَطَأً سِرًّا أَوْ عَلَانِيَةً، وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ مِنَ ٱلذَّنْبِ ٱلَّذِي أَعْلَمُ وَمِنَ ٱلذَّنْبِ ٱلَّذِي لَا أَعْلَمُ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ عَلَّامُ ٱلْغُيُوبِ وَسَتَّارُ ٱلْعُيُوبِ وَغَفَّارُ ٱلذُّنُوبِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّٰهِ ٱلْعَلِيِّ ٱلْعَظِيمِ</p>

**Transliteration:** Astaghfirullah rabbi min kulli dhanbin adnabtuhu amadan aw khataan sirran aw alaniyah, wa atubu ilayhi minadhdhanbilladhi aalamu wa minadhdhanbilladhi la aalamu, innaka anta allamul ghuyubi wa sattarul uyyubi wa ghaffarudh dhunubi wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil aliyyil azim.

**English Meaning:** I seek forgiveness from Allah, my Lord, from every sin I committed knowingly or unknowingly, secretly or openly, and I turn to Him in repentance from the sin I know and the sin I do not know. Surely You are the Knower of the unseen, the Concealer of faults, the Forgiver of sins. And there is no power and no strength except with Allah, the Exalted, the Mighty.

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Allah is more pleased with the repentance of His servant than one of you who finds his lost camel in a waterless desert." (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)

## Sixth Kalima: Radd-e-Kufr (The Word of Rejection of Disbelief)

The Kalima Radd-e-Kufr explicitly rejects major forms of disbelief and affirms faith in Allah alone.

<p className="io-arabic">ٱللَّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ أُشْرِكَ بِكَ شَيْئًا وَأَنَا أَعْلَمُ بِهِ، وَأَسْتَغْفِرُكَ لِمَا لَا أَعْلَمُ بِهِ، تُبْتُ عَنْهُ وَتَبَرَّأْتُ مِنَ ٱلْكُفْرِ وَٱلشِّرْكِ وَٱلْكِذْبِ وَٱلْغِيبَةِ وَٱلْبِدْعَةِ وَٱلنَّمِيمَةِ وَٱلْفَوَاحِشِ وَٱلْبُهْتَانِ وَٱلْمَعَاصِي كُلِّهَا، وَأَسْلَمْتُ وَأَقُولُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ</p>

**Transliteration:** Allahumma inni auzubika min an ushrika bika shay-an wa ana alam bihi, wa astaghfiruka lima la alam bihi, tubtu anhu wa tabarartu minal kufri wash shirki wal kidhbi wal gheebati wal bidaati wan nameemati wal fawahishi wal buhtani wal maasi kulliha, wa aslamtu wa aqulu la ilaha illallah muhammadur rasulullah.

**English Meaning:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from knowingly associating anything with You, and I seek Your forgiveness for what I do not know. I repent from it and I declare myself free from disbelief, polytheism, falsehood, backbiting, innovation, tale-bearing, immorality, slander, and all sins. I submit and I say: There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.

This kalima reflects the Quranic command:

> "And say, My Lord, I seek refuge in You from the incitements of the devils. And I seek refuge in You, my Lord, lest they be present with me." (Quran 23:97-98)
